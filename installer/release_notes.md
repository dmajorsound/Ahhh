<h2 align="center">Ahh!</h2>
<p align="center"><i>Room Simulation</i></p>

Ahh places a sound in a room: source, listener, walls, barrier and motion, for film sound and animation.

### Download
- **Mac** (Apple Silicon and Intel): `Ahh_FILEVER_installer_mac.pkg` below (VST3, AU, CLAP, each optional)
- **Windows** (64-bit): `Ahh_FILEVER_installer_win64.exe` below (VST3, CLAP, each optional)

### Install
1. Quit REAPER.
2. Open the installer and click through. It puts Ahh into the common plug-in folders.
3. Start REAPER. If Ahh does not show up: **Options → Preferences → Plug-ins → VST → Re-scan**.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Mac: "Ahh can't be opened because it is from an unidentified developer"**
>
> The installer is not signed by Apple. Do **not** double-click it again. Instead:
> 1. **Right-click** (or Control-click) the .pkg and choose **Open**.
> 2. Click **Open** in the warning.
>
> Still blocked? Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Ahh.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Windows: "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen)**
>
> The installer is not signed by Microsoft.
> 1. Click **More info**.
> 2. Click **Run anyway**.
>
> If your browser blocks the download, choose **Keep** in the download list.
