<h2 align="center">Ahh!</h2>
<p align="center"><i>Room Simulation</i></p>

Ahh places a sound in a room: source, listener, walls, barrier and motion, for film sound and animation.

### Download
- **Mac** (Apple Silicon and Intel): `Ahh_FILEVER_installer_mac.dmg` below (VST3, AU, CLAP, each optional)
- **Windows** (64-bit): `Ahh_FILEVER_installer_win64.exe` below (VST3, CLAP, each optional)

### Install
1. Quit your DAW first.
2. **Mac:** open the .dmg and double-click **Install Ahh**. **Windows:** open the installer. Click through; it puts Ahh into the common plug-in folders.
3. Start your DAW. If Ahh does not show up, rescan your plug-ins there.

### Uninstall
- **Mac:** quit your DAW, open the .dmg and double-click **Uninstall Ahh** (also below as `Ahh_FILEVER_uninstaller_mac.pkg`).
- **Windows:** quit your DAW, open `Ahh_FILEVER_uninstaller_win64.exe` and click through. Or **Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Ahh → Uninstall**.

The uninstaller also removes Roomservice, as the plug-in was called up to version 1.0.4. Your projects and presets stay untouched.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Mac: "Install Ahh can't be opened"**
>
> The installer is free and not signed by Apple, so macOS asks once:
> 1. Click **Done** in the warning.
> 2. Double-click **Blocked? Open Privacy & Security** in the .dmg window, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
> 3. Double-click **Install Ahh** again and choose **Open**.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Windows: "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen)**
>
> The installer is not signed by Microsoft.
> 1. Click **More info**.
> 2. Click **Run anyway**.
>
> If your browser blocks the download, choose **Keep** in the download list.
